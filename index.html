First Look

Pause at the doorway. Assess the whole room before approaching beds.

A · Room ID & Privacy
All resident names displayed on the room door are accurate.
No clinical or personal care information is visibly posted where it could compromise resident privacy.
B · General Room Condition
Walking paths are clear and free of unnecessary clutter or trip hazards.
Room is free of strong or unpleasant odor.
Room temperature appears comfortable.
Walls, doors, trim and visible surfaces are free of significant damage.
Floors appear clean, dry and free of visible debris or sticky areas.
No unnecessary linens or facility supplies left exposed in the room.
Privacy curtains are clean, intact and appropriately positioned.
Bed linens and pillows appear clean and in good condition.
C · Furniture & Supplies
Closets and dressers appear in good repair.
Room furniture appears safe and in good repair.
Gloves are stocked.
